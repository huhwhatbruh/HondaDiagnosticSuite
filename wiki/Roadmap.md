# Roadmap

Order, not dates.

1. **First public release (read-only).** Scan, codes, live data, history, health report, export.
2. **Opt-in diagnostics sharing** goes live once the collection endpoint is deployed.
3. **More cars.** Driven by vehicle reports.
4. **Other platforms.** Windows and macOS builds.
5. **Actuator tests and writing functions**, only after they have been checked on a real car with the safeguards in
   [Safety](Safety.md).

This page will change as reports come in.
