# Code of conduct

This project is for people who want to understand and repair their own cars. Everyone taking part, in issues, pull requests, the
wiki or anywhere the project is discussed, is expected to:

- **Be respectful.** Disagree with the idea, not the person. No harassment, insults or discrimination.
- **Be specific and patient.** Many people here are owners, not programmers. Explain what you mean; assume good faith.
- **Keep private things private.** Do not post anyone's VIN, location or personal data, including your own, in a public thread.
- **Keep it safe.** Do not encourage anyone to send untested commands to a car, or to use the tool on a vehicle that is not theirs.

The maintainer may edit or remove comments, and block people who do not follow this. To report a problem, use GitHub's private
reporting described in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), or contact the maintainer through their GitHub profile.

This text is short on purpose. Where it is silent, the [Contributor Covenant 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
is the intended reading.
